**1. The "Furniture Trap" (Profitability vs. Revenue)**
- KPI Involved: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Profit Margin %
- The Insight: While Furniture generates significant revenue (often 2nd highest), it consistently drags down overall profitability due to high shipping costs and deep discounts.
- The Data: Furniture likely shows a Profit Margin < 10% (vs. Technology’s ~20%). In some regions (like the West), Furniture may even show negative total profit.
- Executive Action: Stop discounting Furniture aggressively. Renegotiate shipping contracts for bulky items or increase prices on low-margin sub-categories like Tables and Bookcases.

**2. The "Small Order" Risk (AOV vs. Negative Profit)**
- KPI Involved: Avg Order Value (AOV), Negative Profit Orders
- The Insight: There is a strong correlation between Low AOV and Negative Profit. Small orders (e.g., buying 1 chair or 1 binder) often cost more to ship and process than the profit they generate, especially when discounts are applied.
- The Data: A high count of Negative Profit Orders will cluster around transactions with Sales < $200.
- Executive Action: Implement a Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for free shipping, or restrict high discounts (e.g., >40%) to orders with an AOV above $500.

**3. Technology is the Profit Engine (Pareto Principle)**
- KPI Involved: Total Profit, Profit by Category (Visual)
- The Insight: Technology (specifically Phones and Copiers) drives the disproportionate share of net profit, despite having fewer total orders than Office Supplies.
- The Data: Technology likely contributes ~40-50% of Total Profit while representing only ~20% of Total Orders.
- Executive Action: Protect this margin at all costs. Ensure stock availability for Phones/Copiers is prioritized over low-margin Office Supplies (like Binders/Paper).

**4. Regional Bleeding (Geographic Risk)**
- KPI Involved: Negative Profit Orders (Map Visual), Total Profit by Region
- The Insight: The West region (or potentially Central, depending on the specific year filter) often shows a concentration of unprofitable orders despite high sales volume.
- The Data: The Map Visual will show dark red spots (high negative order count) in specific states (e.g., California or Texas), dragging down the regional Total Profit.
- Executive Action: Investigate logistics costs in the West. Is the discount policy too loose for Western sales reps? A targeted review of Western pricing strategy could recover significant lost profit.

**5. Seasonality & Growth Trajectory (Time Intelligence)**
- KPI Involved: Total Revenue, YoY Growth %, Revenue Trend (Line Chart)
- The Insight: Revenue shows consistent Q4 spikes (holiday season), but YoY Growth % reveals whether we are actually expanding or just riding seasonal waves.
- The Data: If YoY Growth % is flat or negative in Q1-Q3, the business is stagnant and relying entirely on Q4 to hit targets.
- Executive Action: Launch mid-year promotions (Q2/Q3) to smooth out cash flow and reduce reliance on the Q4 holiday rush.
